AI-Assisted Speaking Training Questionnaire

I. AI-assisted speaking training affordances (AISTA, 6 items)

1. When practicing speaking with AI, I can actively control the direction and pace of the conversation. 2. The interaction with AI is bidirectional and continuous; the system responds promptly to what I say. 3. AI provides immediate feedback (e.g., error correction, reformulation, or suggested expressions). 4. The AI dialogue adjusts its difficulty according to my proficiency level, so it is neither too hard nor too easy. 5. Conversations with AI resemble authentic communicative situations (e.g., clear roles and task goals). 6. Using AI enables me to organize collaborative or role-playing speaking practice (e.g., switching roles or simulating scenarios).

II. Flow Experience (FE, 6 items)

7. I am fully concentrated when practicing speaking with AI. 8. I often lose track of time during the practice. 9. I feel that my speech flows smoothly. 10. I clearly know what I need to do next. 11. Although the tasks are challenging, I still feel in control. 12. (Reverse-coded) During practice, I am easily distracted by worries or nervousness.

III. Willingness to Communicate (WTC, 5 items)

13. In conversations with AI, I am willing to take the initiative and expand my responses. 14. In English classes, I am willing to volunteer for short speeches (e.g., selfintroductions or expressing opinions). 15. During group discussions, I am willing to be the first to speak. 16. I am willing to communicate spontaneously in English even with unfamiliar people. 17. When the teacher asks a question, I am willing to raise my hand and answer in English.

IV. Learner Agency (LA, 5 items)

18. I take the initiative to ask AI more challenging questions to advance my learning. 19. I request different types of feedback or examples to better meet my current learning needs. 20. When the interaction is unsatisfactory, I adjust my strategies and guide the conversation (e.g., rephrasing prompts or changing tasks). 21. I transfer what I have practiced with AI to real communication (e.g., preparing to talk with peers or foreign teachers). 22. I make suggestions to teachers or classmates on how to better integrate AI into speaking practice.

一、人工智能辅助口语训练给养 AISTA（6 题）

1. 使用 AI 练口语时，我能主动控制对话的方向与节奏。

2. 与 AI 的互动是双向且连续的，我说的内容会被系统及时接续。

3. AI 能即时给出反馈（例如改错、重述、建议用语）。

4. AI 的对话能根据我的水平调整难度，让我既不太难也不太易。

5. 与 AI 的对话贴近真实交流情境（如情景角色、任务目标明确）。

6. 使用 AI 让我能够组织协作/角色扮演式的口语练习（如轮换身份、模拟场景）。

二、心流体验 FE（6 题）

7. 与 AI 练口语时，我全神贯注。

8. 练习过程中我常常忘记时间。

9. 我感觉表达进行得很顺畅。

10. 我清楚知道下一步要做什么。

11. 虽然有挑战，但我始终感觉在掌控之中。

12. （反向）练习时我容易被担忧/紧张打断（反向计分）。

三、二语交际意愿 WTC（5 题）

13. 在与 AI 的对话中，我愿意主动开口并扩展回答。

14. 在英语课堂上，我愿意自愿进行简短发言（如自我介绍/观点陈述）。

15. 小组讨论时，我愿意首先发言。

16. 面对不熟悉的人，我也愿意用英语即兴交流。

17. 教师提问时，我愿意主动举手用英语作答。

四、学习者能动性 LA（5 题）

18. 我会主动向 AI 提出更有挑战的问题来推进学习。

19. 我会请求不同类型的反馈或示例，以更好满足当前学习需要。

20. 当互动不理想时，我会调整策略并引导对话（如改写指令、换任务）。

21. 我会把 AI 的练习迁移到真实交际（如准备并尝试与同伴/外教交流）。

22. 我会向老师/同伴提出建议，帮助更好地把 AI 融入口语训练。
